The person behind the projects

A little about me.

Hi, I'm Kavya. Glad you found this space!

With a background in Computer Science and a master's in Bioinformatics, my interests sit somewhere between biological questions and computational problem solving. I've always been more interested in understanding what we can do with biological data than simply collecting it.

I'm currently exploring computational biology, cancer genomics, and AI through independent research, hands-on projects, and collaborations. I particularly enjoy turning complex scientific ideas into something understandable, useful, and occasionally interactive.

So, what's The KK Lab?

The KK Lab is simply my corner of the internet.

I wanted a place to bring together the things I'm working on without having to squeeze everything into a resume or a LinkedIn update.

Here you'll find my computational biology projects, research notes, small interactive tools, and occasional writing about things I've tried or found interesting.

It's also a place where I can experiment with ideas, revisit things I've learned, and hopefully make a few scientific concepts more approachable for anyone curious enough to explore.

What am I working towards?

I'm interested in pursuing further research in computational biology, particularly where computer science, genomics, and machine learning overlap.

For now, I'm focusing on building stronger technical skills, gaining practical research experience, and putting together work that reflects what I actually know and what I'm still learning.

This website will probably evolve as I do. Some pages will be polished, some will be works in progress, and there will almost certainly be a few experiments that seemed like a great idea at 11 PM.

That's part of the process.

Thanks for stopping by. Feel free to look around!
